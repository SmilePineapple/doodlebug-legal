Common questions

How do I draw a picture?

Choose a picture on the home screen, then pick Step by Step to follow a glowing guide one line at a time, or Trace to draw over the whole faint picture. Free Draw gives you a blank page.

Where are my finished drawings?

In My Drawings, on the home screen. They are stored on your device only. From there a grown-up can save a picture to Photos, share it or print it.

Why are some drawings locked?

A selection of drawings is free. A one-time purchase unlocks the rest. There is no subscription.

How do I restore my purchase?

Tap any locked drawing, then Restore my purchase. You need to be signed in with the same Apple ID that bought it. Family Sharing is supported.

What is the grown-up check?

A simple times-table question that appears before sharing, the first save to Photos, and purchases. It keeps small fingers from doing these by accident.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Everything except buying or restoring the unlock works offline.

The read-aloud voice is silent.

Check that Voice is switched on in the top corner of the screen and that your device is not on silent.

Which devices work?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or later.

Still stuck? Open an issue at github.com/SmilePineapple/doodlebug-legal/issues and tell us your device and iOS version. Please do not include personal information in a public issue.
